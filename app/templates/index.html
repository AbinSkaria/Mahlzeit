How it works

  1. 1

    Tell us a little

    Three short pages: a bit about you, which Mensa and times suit you, and who you'd like to meet. No name needed.

  2. 2

    We set the table

    Every week we match groups of four with shared lunch times and something in common. You get an email with your table number.

  3. 3

    Show up and eat

    Find your numbered table at the Mensa. Conversation starters are waiting. Afterwards, tell us how it went if you like.

What's in it for you

New to Germany?

  • Meet students who actually live here, not just other internationals
  • Ask the questions nobody answers in the welcome week
  • Practice German at your own pace, or stick to English

Grew up here?

  • Practice your English before an exchange semester or job interview
  • Your third lunch is on us
  • Collect a certificate for your CV after three lunches

Don't want to go alone? Sign up together with a friend. You two stay together, and we match you with another pair.

Your data, kept small

No names: at the table you're a random Lunch ID like #K7P2QX. We store your university email (to sign in), your Mensa, lunch times, languages, age group and this-or-that picks. Everything is deleted at the end of the semester. Read the details
