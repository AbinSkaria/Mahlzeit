Privacy at Mahlzeit!

What we store

  • Your university email address (only to sign you in and send invitations)
  • A random Lunch ID (e.g. #K7P2QX), shown instead of your name
  • Newcomer or local, faculty, languages and your this-or-that picks
  • Your chosen Mensa and lunch times
  • Your age and the age range you'd like at your table (your table never sees your age)
  • Optionally: a friend's email, your feedback after a lunch, and any report you send

We never ask for your name, nationality, date of birth, photo, phone number or dietary needs. The Lunch ID is random and not derived from your email. Because we still keep your email for signing in, your data is pseudonymous rather than fully anonymous.

Who sees it

Your table sees only your Lunch ID and whether you're new to Germany or local. Your email is shared with your table only if everyone at it says they'd like to stay in touch. Feedback is seen only by the Fit4Germany team. The team can see the data to run the matching.

Reports

If you report someone, they are never told who reported them. The team sees reports with Lunch IDs only. If the team needs to follow up, they can reveal the email of the reported person; every reveal is logged. Your own email is only revealed if you agreed to be contacted. The two of you are never seated together again.

Legal basis and deletion

We process your data based on your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR). You can withdraw it at any time by deleting your account. All data is deleted automatically at the end of each semester.

Where it lives

On a THWS server in Germany. No tracking, no analytics, no third-party fonts or scripts.
